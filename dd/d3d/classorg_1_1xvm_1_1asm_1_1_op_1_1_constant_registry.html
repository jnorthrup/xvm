◆ m_mapConstants

Map<Constant, Integer> org.xvm.asm.Op.ConstantRegistry.m_mapConstants
private

The constants registered in the ConstantRegistry.

While the registry is still registering, the map tracks counts of how many times each constant is referenced. Once the registry optimizes its constant ordering, the map holds the index of each constant (in the local "constant pool", not the real constant pool.)
