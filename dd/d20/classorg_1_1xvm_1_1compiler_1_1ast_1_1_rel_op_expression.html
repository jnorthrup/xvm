Detailed Description

Relational operator expression (with @Op support) for something that follows the pattern "expression operator expression".

  • COND_XOR: "^^" -
  • BIT_OR: "|" -
  • BIT_XOR: "^" -
  • BIT_AND: "&" -
  • DOTDOT: ".." -
  • SHL: "<<" -
  • SHR: ">>" -
  • USHR: ">>>" -
  • ADD: "+" -
  • SUB: "-" -
  • MUL: "*" -
  • DIV: "/" -
  • MOD: "%" -
  • DIVREM: "/%" -
