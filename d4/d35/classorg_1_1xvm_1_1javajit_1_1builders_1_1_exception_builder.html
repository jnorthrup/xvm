Detailed Description

The builder for Exception types.

For every natural subtype "XYZ" of "ecstasy.Exception" this builder creates a method that overrides the $createJavaException(Throwable cause) method at "org.xtclang.ecstasy.Exception" class.
In addition, this builder generates the "e$XYZ" class of Exception shape via assembleJavaException method.
