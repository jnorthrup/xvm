Detailed Description

Generic expression for something that follows the pattern "expression operator expression".

  • COLON: ":" - an "else" for nullability checks
  • COND_ELSE: "?:" - the "elvis" operator
  • COND_OR: "||" -
  • COND_XOR: "^^" -
