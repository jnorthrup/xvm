Detailed Description

The Zone enumeration defines the zone within which a particular type is declared.

  • TopLevel - the module itself, or declared within a module or package;
  • InClass - declared within a class, e.g. an inner class;
  • InMethod - declared within the body of a method.
  • InProperty - declared within the body of a property.
