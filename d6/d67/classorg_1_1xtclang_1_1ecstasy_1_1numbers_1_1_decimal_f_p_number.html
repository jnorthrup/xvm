int org.xtclang.ecstasy.numbers.DecimalFPNumber.$compareForObjectOrder ( DecimalFPNumber that)

Compare this decimal to another decimal for purposes of ordering.

Note that this does not strictly order by the numeric value of the decimal itself, since two decimals may have the same numeric value, but differ in small ways, such as defined by section "3.5.1 Cohorts".
