A cache of "instantiate-able" ClassCompositions keyed by the "inception type".

Any ClassComposition in this map is defined by a ClassConstant referring to a concrete natural class. It also keeps the secondary map of compositions for revealed types.

◆ f_pendingWorkCount

final AtomicLong org.xvm.runtime.Container.f_pendingWorkCount = new AtomicLong()
private

A counter tracking both the number of services which have pending invocations to process and the number of registered Alarms.

While this count is above zero the container is considered to still have work to do and won't auto-shutdown.
