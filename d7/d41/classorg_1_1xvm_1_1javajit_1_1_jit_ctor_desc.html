Detailed Description

JIT specific information for a constructor.

According to the step 6 of the "CommonBuilder.assembleNew" algorithm, the constructor signature is: construct$17(ctx, cctx, thi$, x, y, z), where "cctx" arg is optional,

We also use this to create the MethodTypeDesc for new$17(ctx, type, x, y, z) function.
